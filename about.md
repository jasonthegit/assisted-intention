Assisted Intention is a running account of an AI fellowship focused on creative generative AI in visual communications education.

The emphasis is practical: graphic design, photography, video, editing, UI/UX, app development, and the question of how these tools can support creative work without replacing human judgment.

This site is built from approved posts only. Raw notes, drafts, screenshots, transcripts, and course materials stay private until they are deliberately prepared for publication.
